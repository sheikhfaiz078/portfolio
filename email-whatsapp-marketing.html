WhatsApp automation and instant response

In the Indian market a lead answered within a minute converts at a completely different rate to one answered the next morning — the person is still on the page, still comparing you against two other tabs. An automated WhatsApp reply closes that gap without anyone needing to be at a desk.

Beyond the first reply, the WhatsApp Business API supports approved template messages for reminders, follow-ups and re-engagement, plus routing so a genuinely interested person reaches a human quickly rather than looping in a bot.

  • WhatsApp Business API setup, verification and approved message templates
  • Instant auto-reply on every form, ad and landing page submission
  • Drip follow-up sequences with sensible timing and easy opt-out
  • Keyword-based routing and handover to a human when intent is high
  • Broadcast campaigns to opted-in segments, within WhatsApp policy

Lifecycle email flows that run themselves

Broadcast newsletters are the visible part of email marketing and usually the least valuable. The revenue sits in automated flows — welcome, nurture, abandoned enquiry, post-purchase and win-back — that trigger from behaviour and keep working long after they are built.

Each flow is written against a specific moment and a specific doubt, then measured on its own. A welcome sequence that lifts reply rate is worth more than a monthly newsletter nobody opens.

  • Welcome and onboarding sequences that set expectations and build trust
  • Lead nurture flows mapped to the objections your sales team hears
  • Abandoned enquiry and abandoned cart recovery
  • Win-back campaigns for leads that went cold months ago
  • Post-purchase flows for reviews, referrals and repeat business

Deliverability, consent and list health

Deliverability is invisible until it collapses. Missing SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, a purchased list, or a sudden send to 20,000 addresses from a cold domain will quietly route your campaigns to spam — and reputation is far harder to repair than to protect.

Consent matters commercially as well as legally. Under India's DPDP Act and WhatsApp's own policy, messaging people who never opted in risks template rejection, number blocking and worse. Lists are built to be legitimate and cleaned regularly.

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured and verified for your sending domain
  • Domain warm-up before any significant volume goes out
  • Opt-in capture, consent records and one-click unsubscribe on every send
  • List hygiene — bounce handling, sunset policies for unengaged contacts
  • Segmentation by source, behaviour and stage so people get relevant messages

Plain-English guide

Owned Channels, Explained.

Why the list you own beats the audience you rent.

Speed is the whole advantage

Every study of lead response says roughly the same thing, and every sales team confirms it: the odds of a meaningful conversation collapse within the first hour. The person enquired because they were thinking about it right then, and they enquired with two other businesses at the same time. Automated WhatsApp reply is not about being clever — it is about being first.

WhatsApp and email do different jobs

  • WhatsApp — immediate, personal, extremely high open rates in India. Right for first response, reminders, confirmations and time-sensitive nudges. Wrong for long-form persuasion or frequent broadcasts.
  • Email — patient, cheap, unlimited length. Right for nurture, education, proof, case studies and win-back over weeks and months.
  • Together — WhatsApp catches the lead while it is hot, email keeps the relationship alive until the person is ready.
  • Never — the same message duplicated across both channels on the same day, which reads as noise and drives opt-outs.

Flows earn more than broadcasts

Most businesses put their effort into the monthly newsletter and never build the sequences. It is usually the wrong way round. A welcome flow, an abandoned enquiry flow and a win-back flow are written once and then work continuously, triggered by what each person actually did — which is why they convert far above anything sent to everyone at once.

Deliverability is a foundation, not an afterthought

If SPF, DKIM and DMARC are not configured on your sending domain, a meaningful share of your email is going to spam regardless of how good the writing is. Blasting a cold domain with 20,000 emails on day one is the fastest way to damage a reputation that then takes months to rebuild. Warm the domain, clean the list, and remove people who have not opened anything in six months — a smaller engaged list outperforms a large dead one.

Consent is now a legal question too

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and WhatsApp's own Business Policy, both require that the people you message have agreed to hear from you. Purchased lists risk template rejection, number blocking and regulatory exposure. Building opt-in properly is slower and it is the only version of this that is safe to scale.

How this connects to your ad spend

Lifecycle automation is the multiplier on everything upstream. If paid campaigns produce the same number of leads but a larger proportion of them are answered, nurtured and eventually converted, your effective cost per customer falls without spending another rupee on ads. That is why automation is built into every performance engagement rather than sold as an unrelated extra.

Questions

Automation Questions.

What is the WhatsApp Business API and do I need it?

The API is the version of WhatsApp Business built for automation and scale — it allows instant auto-replies, approved template messages, routing and CRM integration, which the free Business app cannot do. If you receive more than a handful of enquiries a day, or nobody is available to reply within minutes, it pays for itself quickly.

Can I send bulk WhatsApp messages to a purchased list?

No, and you should not want to. WhatsApp requires opt-in, and messaging people who never consented leads to template rejection and number blocking — plus real exposure under India's DPDP Act. I build opt-in capture into your forms and ads so the list you grow is one you can actually message.

Which email platform do you work with?

Whatever suits your size and budget — Mailchimp, Brevo, Klaviyo, Zoho Campaigns or HubSpot. Everything is built in your own account so you keep the flows, templates and contacts. If you have no platform yet, I will recommend one based on your list size and whether you sell products or services.

Why do my emails land in spam?

Almost always one of four things: missing SPF, DKIM or DMARC records; sending large volume from a domain with no warm-up history; a list with old or purchased addresses generating bounces and complaints; or content patterns that trigger filters. The audit identifies which apply to you and the fixes are part of the setup.

How quickly can WhatsApp automation go live?

The build itself is fast — usually a few days. The variable is Meta's business verification and template approval, which can take anywhere from a couple of days to a couple of weeks depending on your documentation. I handle the submission and keep it moving.

Will this work alongside my ads?

That is exactly where it belongs. Automation is what stops paid leads going cold, so it is included inside performance marketing retainers as standard. Running ads without a fast follow-up system is paying full price for leads and then using a fraction of them.
