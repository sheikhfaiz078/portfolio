## ✈️ HOW WE GET THERE — ROUTE DECISION (research summary)

I compared the three routes you asked about:

| Route | Flight | Road leg to Kasol | Total door-to-Kasol | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mumbai → Chandigarh → Kasol** | 2h 10m **direct**, ~₹7,000–8,000/leg (Oct peak; less if booked early) | ~290 km / 8–9 h | ~11–12 h | ✅ **BEST balance** of cost, comfort, time |
| Mumbai → Delhi → Kasol | ~2h direct, often ₹5,000–6,000/leg (cheapest) | ~520 km / 12–14 h | ~15–16 h | 💰 Cheapest flight, but brutal road leg eats a full day/night |
| Mumbai → Bhuntar (Kullu) → Kasol | **No direct flight**, 1-stop ~5 h, ~₹19,600/leg | ~30 km / 1 h | shortest road | ❌ Flight far too expensive for this budget |

**DECISION: Fly Mumbai ⇄ Chandigarh (direct), then road to Parvati Valley.**
Chandigarh gives you a short direct flight *and* a manageable 8–9 h mountain drive (vs. 12–14 h from Delhi), which matters a lot for a couple who wants comfort. Bhuntar would be ideal on distance but the connecting fare (~₹19.6k one way) blows the budget.

> 💡 **Money-saver alternative:** If Chandigarh fares are above ~₹8,000/leg when you book, fly **Mumbai → Delhi** (cheaper) and take an **overnight Volvo Delhi → Bhuntar/Manali**. Saves ~₹10,000+ on flights but adds ~4–5 h of road each way. Use only if the budget is tight — otherwise Chandigarh is nicer.

**⚠️ VERIFY BEFORE BOOKING:** All flight fares are estimates for October peak season. Book **60–75 days early on a weekday** for the lowest price. Do NOT treat these as confirmed timings — check IndiGo / Air India / Air India Express live.

## 🌦️ WEATHER — EARLY OCTOBER 2026 (what to actually expect)

- **Kasol / Manali days:** pleasant, ~15–22°C, mostly clear post-monsoon skies — the *best* light of the year for photography. 🍂
- **Nights:** cold, ~5–10°C (Kasol) and ~4–8°C (Manali). Carry a proper jacket.
- **Sissu / Lahaul (Day 5):** colder, ~2–12°C, windy.
- **Snow:** Early October is usually **NOT** snowy. First snowfalls typically arrive **late** October. **Do not expect snow, and do not book any "snow activity."** If you get a fresh dusting on the high peaks, treat it as a lucky bonus.
- **Rohtang Pass:** weather-dependent, closes for the season mid-Oct to mid-Nov, needs an online permit, and shuts on Tuesdays. **We are NOT relying on Rohtang** (see Day 5).

**⚠️ VERIFY BEFORE BOOKING:** Check road status for Chandigarh–Kullu highway and Atal Tunnel a few days before travel; mountain weather changes fast.

# DAY 5 — 5 OCTOBER (Monday)
## MANALI → SOLANG → ATAL TUNNEL → SISSU — *The most scenic day*

> The showpiece day. **Atal Tunnel + Sissu is the chosen route** — here's why, and the full plan.

### 🔍 OPTION COMPARISON (for 5 Oct 2026)
| Option | Status early Oct | Snow? | Permit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solang Valley** | ✅ Open, easy | Unlikely | None | Great for adventure/views — we include it *en route* |
| **Atal Tunnel + Sissu** | ✅ **All-weather, open year-round** | Maybe a dusting on peaks | None | ✅ **CHOSEN** — dramatic tunnel, Lahaul valley, Sissu waterfall & river |
| **Rohtang Pass** | ⚠️ Weather-dependent, closing soon, **online permit needed, shut Tuesdays** | Uncertain | Yes | ❌ Too unreliable — do NOT bank on it |

**DECISION: Manali → Solang (photo stop) → Atal Tunnel → Sissu → back to Manali.**
Atal Tunnel is all-weather (no snow-closure risk), needs **no permit**, and drops you into the stark, gorgeous **Lahaul valley** — a completely different landscape from green Manali. This combo gives you Solang's alpine meadow *and* Sissu's snow-peak-backed river village in one day. **We do NOT promise snow** — early Oct is usually clear; any snow on the high peaks is a bonus.

### 📍 LOCATION
Manali → Solang Valley → **Atal Tunnel (9.02 km)** → **Sissu (Lahaul)** → return Manali.

### 🕐 TIMELINE
- **07:30 AM —** ☕ Early breakfast (pack a light layer + sunglasses; it's colder up top).
- **08:30 AM —** 🚗 Depart Manali.
- **09:15 AM —** **Solang Valley** — photo stop, meadow & peak views; optional short ropeway/ATV *(OPTIONAL, see costs)*.
- **10:30 AM —** Drive to the **Atal Tunnel** — the 9 km engineering marvel under the mountains.
- **11:15 AM —** Exit into **Lahaul** — pull over at the **Sissu viewpoint** for the valley reveal. 😍
- **11:45 AM —** **Sissu village:** **Sissu waterfall**, the Chandra river bank, **Sissu lake/helipad ground**, snow-peak backdrop.
- **01:30 PM —** 🍽️ **Lunch in Sissu** (small dhabas — hot thukpa/Maggi/rajma-chawal).
- **02:30 PM —** More photos / riverside relaxing at Sissu.
- **03:30 PM —** Begin return through the tunnel.
- **04:30 PM —** Optional final **Solang sunset** photo stop.
- **06:00 PM —** Back in Manali, rest.
- **08:00 PM —** ❤️ **Romantic candle-lit dinner** — treat yourselves tonight (Johnson's Café garden / Il Forno / a valley-view rooftop).
- **10:00 PM —** Couple time / stargazing — Lahaul-clear skies permitting.

### 🚗 TRAVEL
- **Route:** Manali → Solang (~14 km) → Atal Tunnel → Sissu (~40 km total one way)
- **Time:** ~1.5–2 h each way (traffic at tunnel possible)
- **Transport:** **Full-day private cab** — the only sensible option up here.
- **Cost (2 pax):** **~₹3,500–4,500** for the day (Manali–Sissu round trip). *Negotiate a fixed full-day rate.*

### 🏔️ SIGHTSEEING
- **Solang Valley** — *why:* wide alpine meadow ringed by peaks; ~45 min; free entry (rides paid); **best photo:** meadow with peaks behind, morning.
- **Atal Tunnel** — *why:* driving through 9 km under the Himalaya is an experience itself; no stopping inside; **best photo:** the south portal sign before entering.
- **Sissu** — *why:* the day's highlight — waterfall, turquoise Chandra river, snow-dusted peaks, huge open valley, far fewer crowds; ~2 h; free; **best photo:** Sissu waterfall + village with peaks behind, midday; and the valley viewpoint just past the tunnel.

### 🍽️ FOOD
- **Breakfast:** Manali hotel (~₹500 for 2)
- **Lunch:** Sissu dhaba — thukpa/momos/rajma-chawal (~₹500 for 2)
- **Café/snacks:** chai at the tunnel/Solang (~₹200)
- **Dinner:** 🌟 romantic finale — **Johnson's Café** garden or **Il Forno** (hilltop Italian) (~₹1,300 for 2)

### ❤️ COUPLE EXPERIENCE
