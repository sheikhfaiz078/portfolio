How Sheikh Faiz works

A Process Built on Evidence, Not Guesswork.

Five stages, run the same way on every engagement — so you always know what is happening and why.

1

Free Audit

Sheikh Faiz reviews your existing ad accounts, tracking setup and landing pages, then tells you plainly what's working, what's leaking budget, and whether he's the right fit.

2

Strategy & Tracking

The numbers that matter are agreed up front — cost per qualified lead, not vanity clicks. GA4, Google Tag Manager and conversion tracking are configured properly before a rupee is spent.

3

Build & Launch

Campaigns, creatives and landing pages are built together so the message stays consistent from the ad to the form submission. Launch is deliberately small and measured.

4

Optimise & Scale

Weekly reviews, structured A/B tests and budget shifted toward what converts. Scale happens only once the unit economics hold.

5

Report Honestly

A clear monthly view of spend, leads and cost per lead — including the things that didn't work and what is changing because of it.

Before you ask

The Questions Everyone Starts With.

The things people want to know before the first call — answered plainly.

What does working together actually cost?

It depends on scope and ad budget, but management sits well below the 30% most agencies charge. The free audit comes first — Sheikh Faiz would rather quote against something real than a guess.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid channels usually show a directional signal within 2–4 weeks and a stable cost per lead by 6–8 weeks. SEO is a 4–6 month commitment. Anyone promising faster is selling you something.

Do you work with businesses outside Mumbai?

Yes. Sheikh Faiz is based in Thane, Mumbai, and works remotely with clients across India and internationally — over calls, shared dashboards and weekly written updates.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Accounts stay in your name and Sheikh Faiz gets manager access. If the engagement ever ends, you keep everything — history, audiences and all.

Can you fix an account someone else set up?

Often, yes — and it is usually where the fastest wins are. Broken conversion tracking and overlapping audiences are the two most common problems found in an audit.

Do you also build the landing pages?

Yes. Design and development are part of the service, which means the ad and the page can be tested as one system instead of blaming each other.
