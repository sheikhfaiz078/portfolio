How Sheikh Faiz works

The Process.

Every project follows a clear, transparent workflow — so you always know what's happening and why.

1

Discovery & Audit

Sheikh Faiz starts with a deep dive into your current marketing — ad accounts, analytics, website, and competitors. A full audit identifies what's working and what's leaking money.

2

Strategy & Roadmap

Based on the audit findings, a custom growth roadmap is built. This includes channel selection, budget allocation, targeting strategy, and KPI targets for the first 90 days.

3

Build & Launch

Campaigns go live — landing pages are built, tracking is configured, ad creatives are tested, and automation workflows are deployed. Fast execution, no delays.

4

Optimize & Scale

Weekly optimization cycles: pause underperformers, double down on winners, test new angles. Sheikh Faiz provides transparent weekly reports with real numbers.

5

Report & Iterate

Monthly strategy reviews with data-backed recommendations. Campaigns evolve with your business — new channels, new creatives, new opportunities.
