What is inside

Built Around Conversation, Not Keywords.

Three things decide whether a ChatGPT Ads account works: the context you describe to the system, how much genuine creative variety you feed it, and whether your landing page is even reachable by OpenAI’s crawlers.

Campaign architecture and context hints

The structure will look familiar — campaign, ad group, ad — but the levers underneath are different. A campaign holds your objective, budget, dates and country targeting. An ad group holds a maximum bid and, crucially, your context hints: short descriptions of the conversations, topics or keywords where your offer is genuinely useful.

Hints are not keywords. They guide matching, they do not guarantee delivery in any specific conversation, and there are no match types to hide behind. So the work moves upstream: understanding the situations your customers are actually in when they open ChatGPT, and describing those situations accurately rather than stuffing a list of product names.

  • Objective chosen deliberately — Views for reach on CPM, Clicks for traffic on CPC, or Conversions optimised toward a tracked event
  • Ad groups split by intent theme so one weak hint cluster cannot drain the whole budget
  • Context hints written from real customer language, then rewritten as delivery data arrives
  • Maximum CPM or CPC bid set per ad group, with Ads Manager bid-strength guidance checked before launch
  • Country targeting and budget pacing configured for the markets you can actually service

Creative volume, exact specs and landing page readiness

There is one ad format: advertiser name, logo, a title, a line of copy, a square image and a landing page. The title runs 16–24 characters as a recommendation and 50 at the absolute maximum; the copy 32–48 recommended and 100 maximum. You get no carousel, no video, no landscape crop. Everything therefore rests on how many genuinely distinct angles you give the system to find opportunities with.

The quiet killer is access. Landing pages must not block OpenAI’s user agents, OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot. A great many sites added blanket AI-crawler blocks to robots.txt over the past two years and will fail ad review without ever understanding why. Checking that is part of the technical SEO work I run before anything goes live.

  • A creative library of many distinct title and copy angles per offering, not fifteen rewrites of one line
  • Square image assets produced to spec — PNG or JPG, no larger than 1200×1200, publicly reachable
  • Brand name and logo completed in account settings, without which ads simply will not serve
  • robots.txt and firewall audit for OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot access
  • Landing pages matched to the ad’s promise rather than pointed at a homepage, built the way I build any conversion page

Plain-English guide

A Practical Guide to ChatGPT Ads.

Everything worth understanding before you approve a budget — sourced from OpenAI’s own documentation.

Where the ads appear, and who sees them

Ads render below ChatGPT’s response, clearly labelled and kept visually separate from the answer itself. Each unit carries the advertiser name, a favicon or logo, a title, a line of copy, an image and a landing page.

They are not shown to everyone. OpenAI does not serve ads to users on Plus, Pro or any Business plan, nor to accounts identified as belonging to users under 18. That is a real constraint: your reachable audience is the free tier. Enormous, but worth knowing before you model returns.

How an ad gets matched

There is no keyword auction in the sense you are used to. The system weighs the context and intent of the current conversation, your landing page, your title and copy, the context hints you supplied at ad group level, and — where the user has ads personalisation enabled — selected signals from their broader ChatGPT experience.

Context hints deserve emphasis because they are the piece people misuse. They describe conversations, topics or keywords where your product may be relevant. They are not exact-match keywords and they do not guarantee delivery in any specific conversation. Treat them as a brief for a relevance engine, not as a targeting command.

Objectives, bidding and the auction

Three objectives are available. Views buys impressions on a CPM basis. Clicks optimises for traffic on CPC. Conversions optimises toward a supported standard conversion event — and note that conversion-optimised campaigns using click billing (oCPC) still charge for valid clicks, not for conversions.

You set a maximum bid at ad group level: a maximum CPM for Reach campaigns or a maximum CPC for Clicks campaigns. OpenAI recommends opening CPC bids around $3–$5, and Ads Manager displays bid-strength guidance to flag bids likely to limit delivery. Selection runs through a relevance-weighted second-price auction, which is another way of saying that a more relevant ad can win at a lower bid — exactly as Quality Score behaves in Google Ads.

Because the format is so constrained, the winning strategy is coverage rather than perfection. OpenAI’s own guidance is explicit about it: build a high volume of genuinely distinct title and copy variations per offering, each introducing a different angle, so the system has more chances to find a conversation where you fit. Benefit-focused copy that explains who the product is for and when it helps outperforms a clever line.

Building campaigns: guided or bulk

Ads Manager Beta supports two workflows. Guided creation walks you through campaign, ad group and ad step by step. Bulk upload takes a completed ad schema template — useful at scale, though it does not currently support product-feed campaigns. In the schema, context hints go in as a JSON array, campaign and ad group names must match exactly across tabs, and a partially successful upload is a genuine hazard: re-running rows that already succeeded creates duplicate ads. Retail advertisers have a third option, a product feed that generates ads from an uploaded catalogue.

Measurement, and why you should not trust one dashboard

Ads Manager Beta reports impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, average CPC, average CPM and conversions, viewable in a table, as trend charts, or as a CSV export. Impressions, clicks and CTR can appear within minutes; spend reporting lags, so a displayed zero does not mean nothing has been charged.

Set up conversion measurement inside Ads Manager, and independently add UTM parameters to every destination URL — they persist on ad clicks, which lets GA4 and your CRM tell you what happened without relying on the platform marking its own homework. That second read is the one I care about, and it is the same discipline I apply to Meta Ads and LinkedIn.

Review, brand safety and policy

Accounts go through verification, and campaigns go through ad review. An ad marked “Not serving” will show a reason on hover; many causes are ordinary edits, though some are policy rejections or unresolved billing and verification steps. On the other side, OpenAI states that it places ads only near conversations that are safe and brand-appropriate, with safeguards intended to keep ads out of sensitive contexts.

One practical warning worth repeating: the country, billing currency and time zone selected when you create an advertiser account cannot be changed later. Get them right, or you will be creating a second account.

Where it fits in a real plan

My honest recommendation for most Indian businesses in 2026 is a contained test that runs alongside, not instead of, existing channels. Capture existing demand with Google Ads, build and retarget with Meta Ads, compound with SEO, follow up with WhatsApp and email automation — then use ChatGPT Ads to reach people mid-consideration, and measure whether those enquiries were genuinely incremental. If you want that whole picture planned together, that is what performance marketing is for.

Questions

ChatGPT Ads Questions.

What are ChatGPT Ads?

ChatGPT Ads are advertisements that appear below ChatGPT responses. Each ad unit contains the advertiser name, a favicon or logo, a title, a line of copy, an image and a landing page. OpenAI’s system selects ads using the context and intent of the current conversation, the ad’s landing page, title and copy, advertiser-provided context hints, and — where ads personalisation is enabled — selected signals from the user’s broader ChatGPT experience. Ads are clearly labelled and kept separate from ChatGPT’s own answers.

Can I advertise on ChatGPT from India?

Yes. OpenAI opened self-serve access to Ads Manager in India in early September 2026, alongside Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, and its help centre now lists around 52 available markets. You create an advertiser account at ads.openai.com, complete business verification, add a billing profile and payment method, then build campaigns. Note that the country, currency and time zone chosen at account creation cannot be changed afterwards, so they need to be right the first time.

Who actually sees ads in ChatGPT?

Not everyone. OpenAI does not show ads to users on Plus, Pro or any Business plan, or to accounts identified as belonging to users under 18. In practice ChatGPT Ads reaches the free tier — still an enormous audience, but a real constraint worth knowing before you budget.

How is targeting different from Google Ads keywords?

There are no match types. At ad group level you provide context hints — short descriptions of the conversations, topics or keywords where your product may be relevant. They guide matching but are not exact-match keywords and do not guarantee that your ad appears in any particular conversation. Beyond hints, your levers are country targeting and the quality and variety of your creative. If you need literal query-level control, that is still Google Ads.

What does it cost to advertise on ChatGPT?

You set a maximum bid at ad group level — maximum CPM for Reach campaigns or maximum CPC for Clicks campaigns — and OpenAI runs a relevance-weighted second-price auction. OpenAI’s documentation recommends opening CPC bids in the $3–$5 range, and Ads Manager shows bid-strength guidance so you can see whether a bid will limit delivery. My fee is separate and is never a percentage of your ad spend.

What are the ChatGPT ad creative specs?

Ad title: 16–24 characters recommended, 50 maximum. Ad copy: 32–48 characters recommended, 100 maximum. Image: PNG or JPG, square, no larger than 1200×1200 pixels, at a publicly accessible URL. Landing page: valid, reachable and not blocking OpenAI’s crawlers. Brand name and logo come from account settings, and ads will not serve until those are complete.

Could my website block ChatGPT ads by accident?

It happens more often than you would think. Landing pages must not block OpenAI’s user agents, OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot. Many sites added blanket AI-crawler rules to robots.txt over the past two years and then cannot work out why ads fail review. Checking and fixing that is part of the technical SEO work I do before launch.

Should I move budget out of Google Ads into ChatGPT Ads?

Not yet, and not wholesale. ChatGPT Ads is a beta product with a young reporting stack, one creative format and no keyword-level control. It is a genuine new intent surface and worth testing with a contained budget alongside your existing channels — not a replacement for a Google Ads account that is already producing qualified leads.

How do I measure conversions from ChatGPT Ads?

Two ways, and you should use both. Configure conversion measurement inside Ads Manager Beta, and add your own UTM parameters to every landing page URL — they persist on ad clicks, so GA4 and your CRM can attribute traffic independently. Ads Manager Beta reports impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, average CPC, average CPM and conversions. Impressions, clicks and CTR appear within minutes; spend lags, so a displayed zero does not mean nothing has been charged.
