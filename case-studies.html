01Google Ads · Performance

Educativo Funnel Scale

The Challenge

Educativo Private Limited was running Google Ads campaigns that generated clicks but very few qualified leads. Their GA4 setup was misconfigured — page_view events were being counted as conversions, giving the team a completely false picture of performance. Meanwhile, the Search Partners network was consuming roughly 95% of the ad budget while producing zero actual enquiries.

The team was frustrated. Money was being spent, the dashboard showed "conversions," but the phone wasn't ringing and forms weren't being filled. They needed someone to cut through the noise and fix the fundamentals.

Sheikh Faiz's Strategy
  • Complete account audit — identified Search Partners as the primary budget drain
  • Rebuilt GA4 conversion tracking from scratch — replaced page_view events with actual form submissions and call clicks
  • Created a focused ad group ("MBA Leads - Final") with 9 high-intent comparison keywords
  • Removed all irrelevant distance MBA terms and built aggressive negative keyword lists
  • Designed dedicated landing pages for each keyword theme with clear CTAs

Before Sheikh Faiz

  • ❌ 95% budget on Search Partners
  • ❌ Fake conversions (page views)
  • ❌ Broad, unfocused keywords
  • ❌ No negative keyword lists
→

After Sheikh Faiz

  • ✓ Budget on Search only
  • ✓ Real form + call tracking
  • ✓ 9 comparison-intent keywords
  • ✓ 200+ negative keywords

02Google + Meta Ads

MBA Ninja Lead Generation

The Challenge

MBA Ninja (mbaninja.in) is a university comparison platform where students compare MBA programmes. The site had decent organic traffic but was struggling to convert paid traffic into actual enquiries. Google Ads campaigns were targeting overly broad terms like "MBA course" — competing with universities spending 50x the budget.

Sheikh Faiz's Strategy
  • Shifted keyword strategy to comparison-intent: "NMIMS vs DPU," "best distance MBA in Mumbai," "MITSDE fee structure"
  • Rebuilt conversion tracking — removed incorrect GA4 events, set up proper GTM tags for form submissions
  • Created dedicated landing pages for each comparison query with fee tables and download CTAs
  • Layered Meta remarketing to recapture visitors who compared but didn't enquire

Before

  • ❌ Generic "MBA course" keywords
  • ❌ Competing with university budgets
  • ❌ Broken conversion tracking
→

After

  • ✓ Comparison-intent keywords
  • ✓ Lower competition, higher intent
  • ✓ Accurate lead attribution

03Meta Ads · Lead Gen

StrongNation Performance Ads

The Challenge

StrongNation had an existing Meta Ads setup but couldn't scale without the cost per acquisition spiralling upward. Every time they increased budget, lead quality dropped and CAC spiked. They needed a systematic approach to scaling that maintained quality.

Sheikh Faiz's Strategy
  • Implemented systematic A/B creative testing — testing headlines, images, and CTAs independently
  • Built lookalike audiences from top 10% customers (highest LTV) instead of all converters
  • Deployed Advantage+ with budget guardrails to prevent overspend on exploration
  • Created a 3-stage retargeting funnel: awareness → consideration → conversion

04Multi-Platform

Young AI Champs Lead Gen

The Challenge

Young AI Champs is a 2-day AI Mastery Programme for commerce college students (BAF/BMS/BCom). The challenge was unique: the decision-makers (parents) and the end users (students) are different people with different motivations. Traditional single-audience campaigns weren't cutting it.

Sheikh Faiz's Strategy
  • Created dual-audience campaigns: career-growth messaging for students, future-proofing messaging for parents
  • Ran Google Search campaigns targeting "AI course for commerce students" and similar long-tail terms
  • Deployed WhatsApp automation for instant follow-up — leads received course details, faculty info, and a booking link within 30 seconds of form fill
  • Built a pre-enrollment nurture sequence with student success stories and programme highlights

05Automation

WhatsApp Nurture System

The Challenge

Across multiple education clients, Sheikh Faiz noticed the same pattern: Google and Meta campaigns generated leads, but sales teams couldn't follow up fast enough. By the time a counsellor called back, the lead had already spoken to a competitor. Response time was killing conversion rates.

Sheikh Faiz's Strategy
  • Built a WhatsApp automation that triggers within 10 seconds of form submission
  • Designed qualification flows: the bot asks 3 screening questions before routing to the right counsellor
  • Created a 5-message drip sequence: welcome → course info → fee details → student testimonials → call booking
  • Integrated with Google Sheets CRM for lead scoring and status tracking
